About me

Ljubisav Matejevic is the founder and CEO of InPerson Group, a strategic conglomerate spanning healthcare, technology, venture capital, and executive coaching. With over 30 years of international leadership experience, he has dedicated his career to building alliances that create lasting value.

Today, Ljubisav leads the InPerson Group ecosystem while serving in several leadership capacities:

  • Advisory Board Member for Globally Operating Companies
  • President — Global Clinical + Care Coordination Forum (GCCCF)
  • Global Keynote Speaker & Author — on healthcare transformation, digital strategy, alliance management, and leadership in the VUCA world

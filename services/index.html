Alliance and ecosystem management

In today’s interconnected business landscape, no organization succeeds alone.

Strategic alliances and partner ecosystems have become the critical success factor for companies facing complex value chains and customer demands for integrated solutions. InPerson Group brings decades of experience in building and managing high-performing alliances, from bilateral partnerships to multi-stakeholder ecosystems spanning industries and geographies.

Four dimensions we evaluate

  1. Trust building: assessing and strengthening the foundation of every partnership through systematic trust development frameworks.
  2. Power dynamics: analyzing asymmetries in resources, influence and strategic positioning to ensure balanced, productive relationships.
  3. Information flow: evaluating forward and backward information exchange to identify communication gaps and optimize transparency.
  4. Network effects: mapping interdependencies and reciprocity to maximize the value each partner brings to the ecosystem.

What we deliver

  • A critical analysis of your current alliance and partnership landscape.
  • Actionable recommendations to strengthen collaboration and trust.
  • Full-service partner ecosystem management, from strategy to daily operations.

Whether you need a strategic review of existing partnerships or end-to-end management of your alliance network, we work as part of your team. Competitive advantage comes from superior alliance execution.

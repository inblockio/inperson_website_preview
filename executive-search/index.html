Executive search

We help you identify, attract and hire leaders, whether you are filling a single position or building an entire leadership team.

How we work

  1. Understand the position. We analyze your organizational structure, culture and business objectives, and the requirements of the position to be filled.
  2. Plan the search. A search strategy built for you, using market research, talent mapping and candidate sourcing to find the best possible candidates.
  3. Assess and select. We conduct initial assessments and screenings and manage the interview and selection process.
  4. Keep you involved. Regular updates and your feedback at every stage, so that the final selection reflects your organizational values, culture and strategic objectives.

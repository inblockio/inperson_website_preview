Eco Bio Croatia d.o.o. Zagreb is a production and distribution company dealing with an innovative, EU patented biopolymer that is suitable as an environment friendly, non toxic alternative to flexible, usually single-use plastics.

This biopolymer is starch based and biodissolvable. In hot water it fully dissolves within less than 1 minute, in cold water and in/on the ground, in the period from 25 days to 6 months, leaving no nano particles that enter the ground, waters and food chain. Bioplastic converters can, with no modification nor investment, start using our granulates and offer their clients far healthier and safer products.

Besides this B2B sales model, we also have B2C, offering foils and bags to companies from various business sectors such as general packaging, retail, medicine, pharmacy, cosmetics, agriculture, etc.

Other final products that can be made from our material include cups, plates, protective gloves, etc.

Anything currently made from thin plastics, can be made from our material. We have a R&D facility, Eko Bio Invest d.o.o Belgrade, Serbia, working closely with IMGGE institute from Belgrade.

Regulatory, quality assurance and awareness raising support is provided by WWF Adria, in Adriatic region.

TPdeux

– Inspired by Shinrin-Yoku. Defined by Science.

TPdeux translates the centuries-old fascination with the atmosphere of the forest into a contemporary product experience.

At its origin is Shinrin-Yoku, internationally known as Forest Bathing: the conscious immersion in the forest environment – its stillness, its air, and its distinctive world of scent.

At the heart of this experience are terpenes.

These naturally occurring volatile compounds are released by trees, herbs, and other plants and play an essential role in shaping their characteristic aromas.

At the same time, terpenes and the experience of natural forest environments have become an increasing focus of scientific research.

It is this connection between nature and science that forms the foundation of TPdeux.

Rather than simply recreating the scent of a forest, TPdeux takes a different approach: interpreting the principles behind its distinctive aromatic atmosphere.

Scientific insights into forest air and characteristic terpene profiles provide the inspiration for precisely developed compositions of carefully selected natural essences.

The first expression of this approach is TP2 – Smart Molecules: a highly concentrated terpene composition inspired by Shinrin-Yoku and created for contemporary living environments.

Just a few drops unfold a complex yet deliberately subtle scent profile, transforming TP2 into more than a conventional room fragrance. Its application becomes a modern ritual – a moment to arrive, to perceive, and to consciously experience the atmosphere of a space.

Positioned at the intersection of Forest Bathing, Science, Contemporary Scent Culture and Modern Design, TPdeux introduces a new interpretation of natural scent environments – created for private spaces, hospitality, workspaces, and selected international partners.
